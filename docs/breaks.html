Formats

Three ways to get in

01

Pick your team

You choose the team you want. Prices vary by team, because the stars and top prospects cost more. It’s first come, first served, so popular teams go fast.

02

Random team

Every spot costs the same. When the last spot sells, the on-screen randomizer assigns the teams. It’s the cheapest way in and the most fun to watch.

03

Divisional

Six spots, one for each MLB division. You get every card for five teams, which makes it the best value on big jumbo and case breaks.

House rules

How we keep breaks honest

Sealed on camera

Every box and case is shown sealed and opened on stream from the shrink-wrap to the last pack.

Spots are paid before we start

Claim by phone, email or at the counter. An unpaid spot goes back on the board 30 minutes before start.

Every card ships

Base, inserts, parallels and hits for your team all ship. We keep nothing from a break.

Randomizer on screen

Random-team breaks use a live, on-screen randomizer after the last spot sells.

18 and older

You must be 18 or older to buy a spot. We’ll ask for a date of birth on your first break.

Shipping every Friday

Your cards go out Friday in a tracked bubble mailer for $5 a week, however many breaks you were in.

Break questions

New to breaks?

Here’s the short version. Luis is happy to walk you through it on the phone.

How does a break work?

A sealed box or case is split into spots, usually one per MLB team. You buy a spot, we open the product live, and every card for your team is yours. On the random-team format, teams are assigned by an on-screen randomizer after the last spot sells.

Do I have to watch live?

No. Every break is recorded, and a replay link goes out the next morning. Your cards ship on Friday either way.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. You must be 18 or older to buy a break spot.

Want a spot on Friday’s case?

Call the shop and we’ll hold your team for 30 minutes while you pay by phone. Must be 18 or older.
